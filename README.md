## 🧠 Overview

Interested in modeling brain dynamics using mathematical and computational approaches, with emphasis on neural systems as dynamical processes.

---

## ⚙️ Ongoing Research

Developing computational models of neuronal and network dynamics using **Brian2, Jaxley & NEURON 9.0.2**, focusing on:

* Dopamine-modulated STDP
* Markov-state modeling of voltage-gated ion channels
* Spatial coding and Topological data analysis (TDA) of neural population dynamics
* Low-dimensional manifold representations and geometric organization of neural state spaces
* Activity-dependent neural dynamics

---

* Attractor dynamics and stability in neural systems
* Synaptic plasticity and neuromodulation
* Spatiotemporal dynamics and information coding in SNNs
* Recurrent circuits and population dynamics
* Ion-channel biophysics and neuronal excitability
* Neural state-space geometry, manifolds and topology
